The château beers have been launched

Four beers have been unveiled at the Château de Durbuy, with Marckloff still in production.

On 30 July 2026, Le Soir published an article on the launch of the new range brewed in the former stables of the Château de Durbuy.

Brasserie du Château de Durbuy now presents four beers: Blonde du Château at 6.4% alc., Bohemian Pilsner at 4.5% alc., Amber Ale at 5.6% alc. and IPA at 5% alc.

Production will remain deliberately limited, in keeping with the rhythm of the estate and the scale of a microbrewery.

Marckloff also remains in production. This historic beer remains an important marker of Durbuy’s brewing memory.

The article also recalls the link with the former stables, La Ferme au Chêne and the local brewing tradition that Brasserie du Château de Durbuy continues today with restraint.
